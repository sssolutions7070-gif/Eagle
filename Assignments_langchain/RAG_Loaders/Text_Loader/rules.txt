1. Students must carry their valid college identity card on campus.
2. Students must attend lectures and practical sessions regularly.
3. Students must maintain at least the minimum attendance required by the college.
4. Students must arrive on time for lectures, examinations, and other academic activities.
5. Students must keep classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and campus areas clean.
6. Students must handle college property, laboratory equipment, and library books carefully.
7. Students must maintain discipline and behave respectfully with teachers, staff, and other students.
8. Mobile phones must be kept silent during lectures, practical sessions, and examinations.
9. Students must not engage in ragging, bullying, harassment, or discrimination.
10. Students must follow examination rules and must not use unfair means.
11. Students must submit assignments, practical journals, and project work before the announced deadlines.
12. Students must follow laboratory safety instructions and wear required protective equipment.
13. Students must obtain permission before organizing events or putting up notices on campus.
14. Students must park vehicles only in designated parking areas.
15. Students must follow official college notices and instructions issued by authorized staff.

Note: Students should consult the official college handbook for the applicable attendance requirements, disciplinary procedures, and examination policies.
