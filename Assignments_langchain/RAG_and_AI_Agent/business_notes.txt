Business Research Notes

Product

SmartHome Hub X1 is a fictional smart-home hub. The product manual says
it supports up to 30 compatible devices, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, mobile-app
remote control, schedules and automation, 7 days of local event history,
and basic device-disconnection alerts.

Proposal

The company proposal repeats the 30-device limit, mobile app, scheduled
automation, and basic disconnection alerts. It additionally proposes a
web dashboard for multiple properties and a 90-day cloud event-history
option. These two additions are planned or subject to approval, not
confirmed current features.

Comparison guidance

When comparing the product manual and company proposal: - Identify
features confirmed by both documents. - Identify features mentioned only
in the proposal. - Clearly label planned features and do not present
them as existing capabilities. - If a detail is absent from both
documents, say the information is not available in the documents.
